# Outreach guide

## The approach before the words

Pick the route the organization itself offers, in this order: a partner or press program, a named professional
address published for that purpose, the contact form, a public professional profile that accepts messages. Never a
private channel, never several channels at once for one first contact.

| Kind | Offer that makes sense | Avoid |
|---|---|---|
| client | a short demo or a trial on their own case, with the problem they showed | a feature list, a discount |
| partner | what their clients gain, how the integration or resale works, a call | asking for exclusivity, promising revenue |
| prescriber | content or a session useful to their members | asking them to promote |
| media | a story tied to what the section covers (news, data, a test unit or access), facts in the body | a press release pasted as the message |

## The message

4 to 7 sentences, in the recipient's language, signed by the owner:

1. An opening that proves you looked: their signal, with its date or its page (« Votre offre d'emploi du 3 novembre
   pour un opérateur de saisie… »).
2. Who writes, in one line.
3. Why it concerns them: the link between their signal and what the product does, with one fact from the profile.
4. The offer, with no strings attached.
5. One clear next step (a link to book, a question to answer).
6. EU B2B email: the sender's identity and one line to opt out (« Si ce n'est pas un sujet pour vous, dites-le-moi
   et je ne vous écrirai plus. »).

Test before showing it: could this message be sent to another organization unchanged? If yes, rewrite it.

No superlatives, no fake urgency, no « I hope this finds you well », no attachment in a first message, no tracking
pixel, no promise of results that the profile does not state.

## Follow-up

One follow-up only, 7 to 10 days after the first message when there is no answer, two or three sentences, adding
something new (a result, an article, a date). Then the contact is closed as « sans réponse ». A reply that declines,
or any opt-out wording, closes it for good: record the opposition in the CRM.

## A reply

Summarize it for the owner in one line, propose the answer (a demo slot from the owner's calendar when a calendar
tool is connected, the information asked), flag what only the owner can decide (price, terms, commitments). Never
negotiate, never promise a date or a feature.
